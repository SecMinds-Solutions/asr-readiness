# Privacy notice

This is the privacy notice of ASR Compass, a free community tool by SecMinds Solutions. The
website shows the same notice at [asrcompass.cloud/privacy](https://asrcompass.cloud/privacy),
linked from every page of the website and the app.

## This website

This website does not sign you in and sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, trackers or
third-party scripts, and loads nothing from Microsoft's sign-in service. Only the app, at its own
address, signs you in. Your light or dark mode choice is kept in your browser's local storage, on
this website and in the app; choosing System under Settings in the app removes it.

## What is stored about you

When you sign in for the first time, ASR Compass stores a User record with exactly these
fields, and nothing else:

- your Home tenant ID: the ID of the Entra tenant your account belongs to;
- your username, first name, last name and email address;
- your Contact consent: whether SecMinds may email you about its other products, with the date
  of that choice.

Everything except the Contact consent comes from your Microsoft sign-in, not from anything you
type. You make the Contact consent choice after your first sign-in, with the box unticked.

## What is never stored

An Analysis runs in your browser. Your Tenant's security data, such as Audit events, paths,
device names, policies and Verdicts, is never sent to SecMinds and never stored, and neither is
which Tenants you analyse. Your sign-in tokens stay in your browser tab and are gone when you
close it. ASR Compass uses no analytics or trackers.

## Why

- To know who uses ASR Compass, and to email you about ASR Compass itself.
- Only with your Contact consent: to email you about SecMinds' other products.

## Who can see it

A small number of SecMinds admins can see every User record, list only the Users who gave
Contact consent, and export the records as a file, for example to email Users who gave
Contact consent. They cannot change or delete your record. Nobody else at SecMinds has access
through the app.

## Where and for how long

The User record is stored in Azure Table Storage in Microsoft's West Europe region, in the
European Union. It is kept until you delete it.

## Your rights

Sign in to the app and open **Settings** in the user menu to:

- see your stored User record;
- withdraw your Contact consent, or give it later; your new choice is stored with its date;
- delete your User record; it is removed from storage at once, and nothing about you remains.
